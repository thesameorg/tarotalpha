How the forecast is computed

Every number on the site comes from the formulas below. They are deterministic and open, and they are not financial advice: the cards are a seeded random-number generator in a costume.

1. The seed and the fixation window

A reading is fixed by four values: the instrument, the anchor, the step and the engine label. The anchor is the open time of the last closed hourly candle, so everyone who opens the same instrument within the same hour gets the same cards, and the reading changes at the next hour boundary.

  • Seed string: asset|anchor_ts|step|engine_version, for example BTCUSDT|1789020000000|1|v2.
  • Hash: xmur3 of the seed string feeds a mulberry32 generator. The candle noise uses a second generator seeded with noise| plus the same string.
  • The engine label (v2) is part of the seed, so a formula change draws new cards for new readings. A stored reading keeps its cards and its 168-candle snapshot; its candles are recomputed with the current formulas whenever the link is opened.

2. The deck

78 cards of the Rider–Waite–Smith deck. Majors are ids 0–21 in the classic order (0 the Fool … 16 the Tower … 21 the World); minors are 22 + suit × 14 + (rank − 1) with wands, cups, swords, pentacles and ranks ace (1) … ten (10), page (11), knight (12), queen (13), king (14).

A step draws three distinct cards: id = floor(r() × 78), a repeat is skipped without spending a second number, and an accepted card is reversed when r() < 0.28. The fan of backs on screen is theatre: whichever back you pull, the i-th pull reveals card i.

3. The readers

A reader is who computes the forecast. Every reader draws the same three cards — the reader is not part of the shuffle seed — and turns them into different candles. It is part of the noise seed instead, so one reading never gives two readers the same forecast, and it is stored with a shared reading, so a link replays the reader its author saw.

  • atr — The Magician. Noise in ATR units: drift, width and jumps are multiples of A. Section 4 has the tables.
  • reversion — Temperance. The price is pulled back to the mean of the last 20 closes: close = o + pull × (mean + shove × A − o) + (r() − 0.5) × v × A. The card sets pull, from 0.1 for the Fool to 0.7 for the Hanged Man, and where the mean itself goes: the Wheel moves it to the current price, Death reflects it to the far side, the Tower and the Sun shove past it for one candle. Character: swings in a corridor, almost no trend.
  • analogy — The Wheel. The last 12 returns are compared with every 12-candle stretch of the snapshot by squared distance in NATR units; the card picks one of the 8 closest, and the eight returns that followed it are replayed on the current price, with their mean removed so a rising week does not tilt every forecast upwards, times the card's gain and sign. Character: a plausible shape, the market repeating itself.

The invariants hold whatever the reader: eight candles per card, each one opening at the previous close, no candle narrower than 0.3 ATR, no price at or below zero, and the same seed always giving the same candles.

4. The ATR reader: from a card to eight candles

A step is 24 hourly candles; card k rules candles 8k … 8k+7. Inputs: the 168-candle snapshot, the forecast candles of earlier steps, the three cards.

  • natr — ATR(14) of the snapshot divided by its last close: the plain mean of the true range over the last 14 candles, no Wilder smoothing, as a fraction of price. Computed once per reading.
  • A — the amplitude unit of a candle: natr × previous close. One ATR is the same fraction of price on every candle, so the price can never cross zero.
  • ma — mean close of the last 24 candles (snapshot plus earlier forecast), once per step; trend — the sign of last.c − close 24 candles back, zero reads as +1; dir starts as trend.
  • m — card weight: 1 for a major, rank / 14 for a minor.

For every candle the card sets drift, v (volatility), jump and pull; the defaults are 0, 1, 0, 0, and after the card's rules v = max(v, 0.6).

A reversed major flips the sign of drift and jump. The regime is dir: only the Wheel and Death rewrite it, and the rewritten dir lives to the end of the step.

Minor (sg = −1 reversed, +1 upright) drift v pull
wands sg · m · 0.5 1.0 0

pentacles 0 0.8 m · 0.3

The candle, everything in units of A:

  • o = last.c — always. There are no gaps: crypto never closes, and the old Tower and Sun gaps now live in the body of the block's first candle as jump.
  • c = o + (jump + drift) · A + (r() − 0.5) · v · A + pull · (ma − o)
  • h = max(o, c) + r() · v · A · 0.6, l = min(o, c) − r() · v · A · 0.6; a candle narrower than 0.3 · A gets both wicks widened to it.
  • t = last.t + 1 hour; the candle becomes last for the next one.

Generator call order per candle: swords take one number for the drift sign, then the close noise, then h, then l. The scale is calibrated so that the mean true range of a forecast candle is about one ATR and no candle is narrower than 0.3 ATR.

5. The day summary

Under the three cards the interface writes one paragraph from the step's numbers:

  • netPct — the last close against the first open, in percent; highPct, lowPct — the extremes from the same open. The paragraph prints percentages only: the unit a reader measures in is a technical fact and lives in the tech panel, not in eleven languages.
  • Direction: flat when the net move is under half an ATR, otherwise up or down. ATR is the engine's own yardstick here whatever the reader, so the wording of a quiet day means the same thing everywhere.
  • The ruling card: the strongest major on the table in the order Tower, Sun, Death, Wheel, Moon, Hanged Man, Fool, then any other major, then the highest-ranked minor; a tie goes to the earlier position.
  • A regime flip is flagged when the Wheel or Death is on the table.
  • The closing sentence is picked from the sum of card ids and orientations, so one reading always reads the same.

6. The prophecy check

When a link is opened after its anchor, the browser fetches the real candles that have closed since and compares each forecast candle with the real candle of the same hour. A hit is a matching sign: (fc.c ≥ fc.o) === (real.c ≥ real.o). Accuracy is hits / compared, interim until all 24 × steps candles have a real pair, and 50 % or more reads as "the prophecy came true". Over many readings it lands, honestly, near one half.

7. What a link stores

The cards drawn per step, the 168-candle snapshot the server took at the moment of sharing, the exchange it came from, the reader that drew it and the engine label. Everything else — forecast candles, summary, accuracy — is recomputed in your browser with the current formulas.

Not financial advice. The formulas above turn a seeded random-number generator into candles that look like a market; they know nothing about the market. The cards agree.
